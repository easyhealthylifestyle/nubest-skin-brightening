    About the word “brightening”

    Here, brightening describes support for a healthy-looking, radiant complexion. It is not about changing natural skin color, and no skin tone is better than another. Skin also depends on sleep, sun protection, hydration, diet and genetics, so a supplement is one part of a routine, not a replacement for skin care or a dermatologist’s advice.
